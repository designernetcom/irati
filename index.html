about irati

Industrial robotics expertise. Practical learning.

IRATI (Industrial Robotics Automation & Training Institute) is an initiative of WO Group. WO Group was formed in 2015 by founder Mr. WAGHCHOURE S. R. and works in the field of industrial robotics and automation.

IRATI was started as a training institute for working professionals and for college students, so that anyone can gain working knowledge of industrial robots and be part of robotics automation. We provide full, professional-level industrial robotics training using an actual industrial-level syllabus — and projects that are actually in running are used during the training period.

robotics installation

From robot installation to production

How a WO robot cell goes from a drawing to a running line — we build the full trial at our premises so the customer requirement is covered before dispatch.

  1. 01 [ step ]

    Planning

    Understanding the application, cycle time and layout before anything is ordered or moved.

  2. 02 [ step ]

    Installation

    Mechanical installation of the robot, controller and cell hardware at your site.

  3. 03 [ step ]

    Configuration

    Controller configuration, I/O mapping and end-effector setup for your process.

  4. 04 [ step ]

    Calibration

    Mastering, tool frames and user frames set so positions are accurate and repeatable.

  5. 05 [ step ]

    Programming

    Motion, logic and I/O programming written for the actual production sequence.

  6. 06 [ step ]

    Testing

    Full trial runs — we build the complete trial at our premises before dispatch.

  7. 07 [ step ]

    Integration

    Integration with the surrounding line and handover, with your staff trained on the cell.
